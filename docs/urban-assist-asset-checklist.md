## Delivery checklist

- [ ] Confirm every filename is lowercase kebab-case.
- [ ] Confirm no image contains baked-in text, logos, prices, or watermarks.
- [ ] Confirm card images are 800 × 600 px and ≤ 60 KB.
- [ ] Confirm banner images are 1680 × 720 px and ≤ 120 KB.
- [ ] Confirm loop posters match the first frame of their videos.
- [ ] Confirm videos are H.264, 1280 × 720, 10–20 seconds, muted, seamless, and ≤ 2 MB.
- [ ] Confirm before/after pairs use identical framing and lighting.
- [ ] Run `pnpm sync:images` after adding or changing assets.
- [ ] Check `/services/<slug>`, subcategory pages, and the home page on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Verify that no subject, face, hand, tool, or important detail is cropped.
